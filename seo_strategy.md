# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages for Everkind Home Care
- Service, about, testimonials, FAQ, careers, contact, and job application pages
- Shared crawlability files and metadata that affect the public site

## Out of scope
- Authenticated admin dashboard under `/admin/**`
- Internal API routes except where they affect public-site crawlability or indexing

## Target audience
- Families and individuals seeking home care in Ireland
- Hospitals and nursing homes seeking staffing support
- Prospective carers and nurses considering employment with Everkind

## Primary keywords
- Home care Ireland
- Home care agency
- Elder care services
- Healthcare staffing
- Care jobs Ireland
